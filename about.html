Our Story

About Us

We started with a small team of passionate individuals based in Scotland, brought together by two shared interests: a love for the gym and a deep appreciation for Hatsune Miku and Vocaloid culture. What began as a niche idea between friends quickly evolved into a focused project—combining performance nutrition with a distinct creative identity.

Each member of the team brings a different perspective, from training experience to design and formulation, but all share the same goal: to create something that feels different from traditional bodybuilding products. Late-night discussions, training sessions, and experimentation shaped the concept into a product that reflects both discipline and individuality.

Our Product

Our product is built for bodybuilders who treat competition day like a performance. Inspired by the high-energy world of Vocaloid culture, our products are designed to help you step on stage with intensity, focus, and presence.

It is a fast-acting pre-competition formula created to be taken just before you go on. It’s designed to support muscle fullness, vascularity, and mental sharpness when timing matters most—helping you look and feel ready under the lights. Lightweight, easy to mix, and formulated for rapid uptake, it fits seamlessly into your final prep routine.

The formula comes in three distinct flavours, each inspired by an iconic Vocaloid: Hatsune Miku, Kasane Teto and Akita Neru. Each option is crafted to match a different taste while keeping the same performance-focused formulation.

We combine functional design with a striking, futuristic identity drawn from the digital idol scene. The result is a supplement that stands apart—visually unique, concept-driven, and built around that final moment before you step forward.
